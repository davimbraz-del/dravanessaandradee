# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS: `index.html` + `style.css` + `script.js`, no frameworks, fonts from Google Fonts. Must deploy as-is on free static hosts (Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages).

## Users

People looking for a dentist in Pernambuco, most often on a phone, arriving from Instagram, a WhatsApp share or a search. Their job: decide whether Dra. Vanessa is someone they trust with their mouth, then start a conversation on WhatsApp to book.

## Product Purpose

One-page presence for Dra. Vanessa Andrade, cirurgiã-dentista (FOP/UPE, CRO-PE 22392), recently graduated. Success is a visitor tapping a WhatsApp button with the pre-filled message "Olá, Dra. Vanessa! Gostaria de agendar uma consulta."

## Positioning

A young dentist with a recent, up-to-date education from one of Brazil's most traditional dental schools (FOP/UPE), offering unhurried, explanatory, humanized care with rigorous biosafety. Tagline: "Mais que tratar dentes, cuidar de pessoas."

## Operating Context

Booking happens entirely over WhatsApp; there is no online scheduling system. The page also shows address, hours, Instagram and an embedded Google Map.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: fixed header, hero, Sobre mim, Serviços (6 cards), Depoimentos (carousel), final CTA band, footer with map; floating WhatsApp button.
- Undecided (placeholders the owner will fill): the six services ([SERVIÇO 1]…[SERVIÇO 6]), WhatsApp number, address, hours, Instagram handle. The WhatsApp number lives in a single constant in `script.js`. Map shows Recife until the address exists.
- SEO: title "Dra. Vanessa Andrade | Cirurgiã-Dentista", meta description, alt text on all images.

## Brand Commitments

- Name lockup: "Dra. Vanessa Andrade" as a text logo in serif.
- Credential line: "Cirurgiã-Dentista • FOP/UPE • CRO-PE 22392"; footer line "Dra. Vanessa Andrade – Cirurgiã-Dentista – CRO-PE 22392".
- Voice: warm, first person, light and welcoming, unhurried ("sem pressa"), explains each step. The "Sobre mim" text supplied by the owner is binding copy.
- Visual constraints the owner pinned (recorded, not expanded here): white/off-white base, beige/sand support, soft warm yellow accent used sparingly, very dark brown text never pure black; serif titles, clean sans body.

## Evidence on Hand

- No real photos yet (portrait and clinic/office). Neutral placeholders until supplied.
- No real testimonials yet. Three example testimonials ship clearly marked as placeholders; never present invented testimonials as real.
- No invented services, prices, years of experience, patient counts or awards.

## Product Principles

1. Every path ends in WhatsApp; the booking action is never more than one tap away.
2. Trust comes from true credentials (FOP/UPE, CRO-PE) and a human voice, not from inflated claims.
3. Phone first: the page is designed for a thumb on a 375px screen, then expanded.
4. Placeholders stay obviously placeholders and trivially replaceable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience spans all ages, including older patients: readable body size, strong contrast, large tap targets, zoom never disabled, motion reduced when the OS asks.
